Innovation Idea
Satark Setu + Pramana Protocol: a single verification layer that authenticates a message and structures it into usable data in the same pass.
Today, a citizen's phone treats a scammer's fake KYC message and a genuine bank's real alert identically — same app, same format, no signal to tell them apart. The same is true for a small business owner: a real buyer's GST invoice and a scammer's fake one arrive through the exact same WhatsApp thread, indistinguishable until it's too late. Every existing solution treats fraud-detection and data-organization as two separate problems, built by two separate systems. Our innovation is recognizing they're the same problem: unstructured, unverified communication is both the fraud vector and the reason MSME revenue never becomes visible to lenders.
The system takes any input — pasted text, a screenshot, or a voice note, in English, Hindi, or Telugu — and runs it through one pipeline: normalize the text (defeating invisible-character and lookalike-letter evasion), match it against real scam typologies (fake KYC, digital arrest, courier scams, refund/QR fraud), check any embedded link against trusted threat-intelligence sources without ever opening it directly, and — if it looks like a business transaction — validate its structural authenticity (GSTIN format, invoice shape). Only a transaction that clears every gate gets structured into a strict, honest financial record. Anything that fails, stops there, with a clear verdict and real advice — never a fabricated "safe" result.
Real-World Problem It Solves

Digital fraud at scale: Indian citizens, especially first-time smartphone users in rural and semi-urban India, lose money daily to KYC scams, digital-arrest calls, courier scams, and fake refund QR codes — often in Hinglish or regional languages that generic tools don't cover.
Credit invisibility for MSMEs: millions of small businesses have real, provable revenue trapped in chat threads, handwritten notes, and unorganized invoices. Because that data is never verified or structured, formal lenders can't see it — and standard accounting tools that try to digitize it can't tell a real invoice from a fake one, corrupting the very data meant to prove creditworthiness.

Impact

For citizens: instant, multilingual, evidence-based scam verdicts that catch what keyword filters miss — without needing digital literacy to use it.
For MSMEs and rural entrepreneurs: a byproduct benefit — simply using the tool to stay safe automatically builds a clean, verified transaction history, turning invisible revenue into bankable data at zero extra effort.
For lenders: transaction records that were authenticated at the point of capture, not cleaned up after the fact — a fundamentally more trustworthy signal for credit decisions than self-reported bookkeeping.
For India's Digital Public Infrastructure goals: it closes the loop between fraud prevention and financial inclusion using the same infrastructure — one verification layer serving both DigiLocker-era trust and Jan Dhan–era access, at population scale, without requiring any new behavior from the user beyond what they already do: forward a suspicious message.
